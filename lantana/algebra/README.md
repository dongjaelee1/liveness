# `lantana/algebra/`

Rocq-level mathematics and the cameras (resource algebras) of the logic: ordered commutative
monoids and their well-founded variant, with the ordinals `Ord.t` as an instance; measures, a
syntax for ordinals interpreted at a layer, and the layers themselves; the cameras for obligation
fuel, witness credits and transfinite later credits; and the existential property of `Ord.t`
needed by the adequacy proofs.

Builds on `ordinal/`, `lantana/prelude/`. Used by `lantana/base_logic/`, `lantana/program_logic/`,
`lantana/trace/`, `lantana_heap_lang/`, `lantana_heap_lang/examples/`.

## Files

In dependency order: each file imports only files above it.

| File | Imports from this directory | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `list_of_sets.v` | — | Lists of finite sets: their union `union_list_of_sets` and pairwise disjointness `disjoint_list_of_sets`, under list and set operations. Used only by `lantana_heap_lang/examples/queue.v`. |
| `ocm.v` | — | Ordered commutative monoids (`OCM.t`, with a total preorder), and two constructions on them: the upward-closed sets generated by one element (`quotient`) and the downward-closed sets (`partition`), used by `bank.v` and `bcred.v`. |
| `wfocm.v` | `ocm.v` | Well-founded ordered commutative monoids (`WFOCM.t`): an OCM with a well-founded strict order `ltR` such that `a < c` iff `a + b ≤ c` for some positive `b`, with well-founded induction (`WFOCM.ind`) and the bundle `WFOCM.pack`. |
| `ordinal_wfocm.v` | `wfocm.v` | The opam ordinals `Ord.t` with the Hessenberg sum `⊕` as an OCM (`ord_OCM`) and a WFOCM (`ord_wfocm`, `ord_wfocm_pack`). |
| `measure.v` | — | Layers in general (`layer_class`: a well-founded order up to an equivalence) and measures: the syntax `measure` (embedded well-founded orders `ms_embed`, `ms_large`, sum, product, exponent, and the extension measure `ms_extend`, notation `▽`), interpreted into `Ord.t` at a layer by `measure_rep`, where `▽` is `η` of the supremum of all measures at lower layers. |
| `layer.v` | `measure.v` | The layers of Lantana: `layer := ord.t`, the ordinals of the copy in `ordinal/`, as an instance of `layer_class` (`layer_layer_class`); the ordinal notations, and `measure_rep_layer_le`. |
| `bank.v` | `ocm.v` | The bank camera over an OCM, used for obligation fuel: a fraction `debt a q` of the debt bounds by `a` the `cash` in circulation; cash is borrowed by raising the debt (`debt_owe`) and paid back to lower it (`debt_pay`). |
| `bcred.v` | `ocm.v` | The bounded-credit camera over an OCM, used for witness credits: the persistent `credit_bound a` bounds by `a` the `credit`s in circulation, with splitting (`credit_split`) and spending (`credit_spend`), which lowers the bound. |
| `auth_mra.v` | — | `auth_mra R`: the authoritative camera over Iris's monotone resource algebra for a relation `R`, with fractional authority `●AM[R] a` and lower-bound knowledge `◯AM[R] b` (`R b a`); the class `auth_mra_class` of relations with the full set of rules, the lexicographic order `lex_R`, and the instance `ordinal_auth_mra_class` for the copy's `ord.t`. Used by the ticket-lock examples. |
| `ordinal_ucmra.v` | — | `OrdUR`: the opam ordinals `Ord.t` with the Hessenberg sum as a discrete unital camera, used for transfinite later credits, and cancellation of the Hessenberg sum (`Hessenberg_add_cancel`). |
| `ordinal_existential_properties.v` | — | The existential property of Transfinite Iris for the type `Ord.t` at the ordinal step-index type `ordI` (an existential over `Ord.t` commutes with step-indexing), used by `lantana/trace/` (`itrprop_fixpoint.v`, `itrprop_model.v`, `guarantee_rec.v`). |

## Notes

- `ocm.v`, `bank.v` and `bcred.v` follow the modules `OCM`, `Fuel` and `CounterRA` of the Lilo
  and FOS developments, as their headers say.
- Two copies of the Ordinal library are used on purpose: fuel and credit amounts are opam
  ordinals `Ord.t`, and layers are the copy's `ord.t`, because layers are instantiated with
  ordinals and their universe must be below that of `Ord.t` (header of `layer.v`).
